🎬 Video settings

🎵 Music video (add your own song)

Add a song from your device and I'll build a beat‑reacting music video from your photos/scenes and export it with the audio — all offline. The video length follows your song. Please use music you own or have the right to use — I can't include copyrighted famous songs.

Dance makes your photo move to the beat — bounce, sway, spin, jump, twist, shimmy, headbang and more, cycling through moves. It's an offline motion effect on your picture, not an AI body that re‑poses limbs (that needs paid cloud AI).

💬 Prompt

Type anything — looks (vintage, cinematic, cyberpunk, neon, sketch…), tweaks (brighter, more color, blur), numbers (brightness 150, increase contrast by 20), transforms (rotate left, mirror), or recolour (change red dress to blue, make the green blue). Typos are OK — I'll figure it out.

🎛️ Adjustments

🎨 Recolor (change a color)

Change one colour to another — e.g. a red dress to blue — while keeping the face, body and expression exactly the same. Only the chosen colour changes; shading and folds are kept. Tip: use Pick from photo to click the exact colour, and lower Match range if skin changes too.

🌈 Match a look (from any photo)

Saw a photo you love on Instagram, Pinterest or the web? Save it, add it here as a reference, and I'll give your photo the same colours & mood — your face and body stay the same. (Offline colour-match — not a full AI face-swap.)
